As part of the **InDemandPH Program of BayanAcademy, in partnership with J.P. Morgan**, our team developed a virtual case study for an **AI-assisted NCR COVID-19 Alert Level Notification System**. We started with DOH quarantine-facility data and analyzed bed and isolation-bed occupancy across NCR cities to identify areas experiencing higher facility utilization.

Using **Python and Google Colab**, we processed and organized the data, compared occupancy levels by city, visualized the results, and applied defined occupancy thresholds to classify conditions from **Safe to Critical**. We then translated these results into an automated notification concept capable of generating **SMS and email alerts**, allowing stakeholders to receive timely information on emerging capacity risks and support proactive decision-making.

I contributed to the **data processing and Python implementation**, while also helping coordinate team progress, track deliverables, and ensure the case study was completed within the project timeline.
